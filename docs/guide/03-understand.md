[how](../../skills/how/SKILL.md) traces behavior. [why](../../skills/why/SKILL.md) investigates motivation from source records, retaining uncertainty. [teach](../../skills/teach/SKILL.md) combines findings into an explanation.

[recall](../../skills/recall/SKILL.md) reconstructs only scoped history. For a specific prior session use [session pickup](../../skills/poteto-mode/playbooks/session-pickup.md). Missing verified transcripts get a labeled digest, not a claim of historical coverage.
